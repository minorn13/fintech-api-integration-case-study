# Digital Account Funding API Integration

**Product Management Case Study | FinTech | Digital Banking | API Integration**

## Overview

This case study demonstrates my approach to leading a digital banking product initiative from an initial business need through product discovery, requirements definition, API integration planning, backlog development, prioritization, and user acceptance testing.

The scenario focuses on improving the digital account opening experience for a regional bank by enabling customers to fund newly opened accounts through an external financial institution.

## The Business Challenge

A regional bank has identified friction within its digital account opening journey.

Customers can successfully open a new checking or savings account online, but funding the account requires additional steps outside of the digital onboarding experience. This creates unnecessary friction, contributes to unfunded accounts, and negatively impacts the overall customer experience.

The bank wants to introduce the ability for customers to securely connect an account held at another financial institution and initiate an opening deposit without leaving the digital account opening experience.

A third-party financial technology provider will supply APIs supporting financial institution search, authentication, account retrieval, and account verification.

## Initial Business Request

The initial request from stakeholders is to:

- Allow customers to search for and select an external financial institution
- Securely authenticate with the external institution
- Retrieve eligible external accounts
- Allow the customer to select a funding account
- Enter an opening deposit amount
- Submit the funding request
- Provide confirmation and transaction status
- Handle authentication failures, unavailable institutions, transaction limits, duplicate transactions, and third-party service interruptions

Leadership has requested that an MVP be defined and prepared for development.

My responsibilities include:
